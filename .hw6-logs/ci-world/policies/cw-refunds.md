# Cartwheel refund policy

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method and arrive in 5 to 10 business days. Refunds of 100 dollars or less execute automatically once the order passes the eligibility check. Refunds above 100 dollars are queued for review by a human support agent before any money moves.
