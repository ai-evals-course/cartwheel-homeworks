# Roles and permissions on Cartwheel

Cartwheel has three roles. Shoppers can see and manage their own orders. Merchants can see and manage orders of their own store, and cannot see other stores' orders or shoppers' other purchases. Support staff can look up any order. The platform enforces these permissions in its systems; support assistants cannot grant exceptions.
