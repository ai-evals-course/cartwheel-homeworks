# Getting help on Cartwheel

The Cartwheel support assistant answers questions about orders, returns, refunds, products, and platform policy, and it cites the policy page behind every policy answer. If the assistant cannot resolve a case it opens a ticket for a human agent, who responds within 24 hours.
